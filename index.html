NHC Staff Christmas Challenge · EdTech edition

Snowball Myth Melter

Two snowballs fall each round. One carries a true statement about education technology, the other carries a myth. Melt the myth with your flamethrower and leave the truth standing.

How to play

  1. Read both snowballs as they fall from the sky.
  2. Move the flamethrower with ← → (or A D), or by moving your mouse over the game.
  3. Fire with Space or a mouse click. Melt only the myth.
  4. Every melted myth bursts open to release one of NHC's Five Pillars as a present, which joins the present shelf at the bottom of the screen. Catch it on your sled on the way down for a bonus.
  5. Each level has 5 rounds. Afterwards, a summary explains why every myth is wrong and every truth is right.

Press P to pause. On a touchscreen, tap or drag on the game to aim and fire.

Scoring

  • Melt the myth+100
  • Melt it early (high in the sky)up to +50
  • Catch a Five Pillars present+25
  • Streak bonus, per myth in a row+10 each
  • Melt the true statement−50
  • Myth hits the ground0
